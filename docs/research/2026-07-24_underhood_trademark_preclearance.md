- **Method:** two independent research agents — (1) live USPTO tmsearch/TSDR/TTABVUE + state SOS deep screen; (2) marketplace/domain/social/common-law sweep. Full agent outputs preserved in session transcript; key findings and citations below.

## Verdict

**Name as-is: effectively blocked for practical purposes.**

1. **§2(d) conflict — the controlling problem:** **DR. UNDERHOOD, SN 99842025**, filed **2026-05-23** (use-based), IC 009 *downloadable mobile app for motor-vehicle diagnostics / troubleshooting / live technician video / AI problem-ID* — same class, same App Store channel, same consumer. App live since May 2026 (id6765977965, v1.0.5 Jun 2026); the underlying Arkansas shop (Good Standing, AR SOS) has used the name since ~2011. Any Garage filing is junior; expect a 2(d) citation or prior-pending suspension once 99842025 is examined (~2027). No variant spelling (Undrhood) or composite reliably cures this.
2. **§2(e)(1) descriptiveness:** "underhood" is a dictionary automotive adjective. USPTO precedent in this exact word: VMAC's first UNDERHOOD application was forced to the **Supplemental Register** (2019); its 2023 Principal registration required **§2(f) "in whole" on ~25 years of use**; Babcox's UNDERHOOD SERVICE likewise §2(f)-in-whole. A new app with zero use lands on Supplemental at best — and 2(d) applies there too.
3. **Live crowd in the word:** VMAC UNDERHOOD (Reg 7241333 Principal §2(f) + Reg 5705505 Supplemental, IC 007 compressors); Babcox UNDERHOOD SERVICE (Reg 5467535, IC 041, **incontestable**); Eastwood UNDERHOOD BLACK (IC 002); Nordstrom's **UNDER THE HOOD** (77250863, IC 041 auto radio/podcast, renewed, §15, and present in the App Store via its companion app); Robinhood UNDER THE HOOD family (IC 009/041, non-auto). Pending to watch: UNDER MY HOOD 99767096 (IC 009/042 oil analysis). 10 dead UNDERHOOD-family marks; TTAB has never seen the word (no litigation history — a mild positive).
4. **Marketplace/domains:** `underhood.com` (registered 1998, dormant), `.app` (live unrelated SPA), `.io` (live fleet-fire-safety company) — all the primary domains are gone; only `underhood.us` / `getunderhood.com` / `underhoodapp.com` / `tryunderhood.com` are clean. Instagram @underhood squatted; TikTok/YouTube clean; X unverified. Findability in-niche is POOR: "underhood app"-type queries surface Dr. Underhood and Babcox's *Underhood Service* ahead of any new entrant; bare "underhood" surfaces zero apps on page one. Minor JA transliteration ambiguity (フード hood/food). App Store likely will NOT tokenize "underhood" to match "under the hood" searches — ASO keywords would need both forms regardless of name.
