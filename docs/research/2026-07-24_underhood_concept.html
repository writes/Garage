UNDERHOOD

Design concept UH-24 · Garage naming workstream · 2026-07-24

Know what's under the hood.

Every car wears paint over its truth. Underhood is the maintenance record that lifts the lid — a layered, inspection-lit logbook where every number is a recorded fact, every correction stays visible, and the whole history opens for the next owner exactly as far as you allow.

Native SwiftUI · dark-first Built on shipped Garage infrastructure No invented health scores Interactive prototype below
UH-A

Design principles

Principle

The Reveal

The signature motion is lifting the hood: surfaces hinge open to expose the layer beneath. Depth is the metaphor — nothing important lives on the paint.

Principle

Inspection Light

One amber lamp in a dark bay. The accent is spent only where attention belongs: due work, drafts awaiting review, the active control.

Principle

Machined

Chamfered corners like milled parts, hairline rules, monospaced tabular numerals. The interface reads like a well-kept tool, not a brochure.

Principle

Facts Only

Every status traces to a recorded fact plus interval math — "last done at 78,400 mi, due in 780." No synthetic health scores, no "verified" theater.

UH-B

Working prototype

Five destinations, live interactions, one fictional car. Everything below actually works — walk it in order:

Pop the hood

On the Hood screen, hit the latch. The vehicle card hinges open and the Systems Bay is revealed beneath it — the app's thesis as a gesture.

Tap a system

Each bay tile is a fact card: last service mileage, interval, due math. Tap one for its history and a direct path to record work.

Scan a receipt

Hit the amber chamfer in the dock → "Scan it." Watch the draft ritual: parsed fields stay amber and uncommitted until you press Commit to Logbook. Then watch the odometer roll and the coolant light go green.

Interrogate the logbook

Filter by type, then expand the brake entry's correction stamp — the superseded mileage stays struck through, never erased.

Build the handover

On Handover, flip the switches — the dossier preview rebuilds live: page counts, record counts, included sections. What leaves your account is exactly what you can see.

UH-D

Information architecture

Tab 1HoodCurrent odometer, service lights, and the hood-lift reveal into the Systems Bay. The state of the machine, at a glance and in depth.
Tab 2LogbookThe full record — searchable, filterable, every entry stamped with its origin, evidence, and any corrections.
Center actionRecordType it, say it, or scan it. Every non-manual path lands as an amber draft that only the owner commits.
Tab 3BayAll vehicles, plus the parts shelf: batteries, tire sets, warranties — the physical stuff the paper trail describes.
Tab 4HandoverThe bounded, owner-controlled dossier. Monetization lives here and at other value moments — never on a locked tab.
UH-E
