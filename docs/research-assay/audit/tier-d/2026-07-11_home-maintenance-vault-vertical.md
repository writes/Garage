## Core claim
Expanding Garage into a home/asset maintenance vault vertical improves TAM and revenue via
homeowners maintaining provenance-priced maintenance records the way car owners do.

## Kill reason
The evidence actively refutes the mechanism, it doesn't merely fail to support it:

- **Named corpse:** Centriq — the consumer home-maintenance app — is dead as of January 2026.
- **Homes fail all three adjacency tests** (VISIONARY #20): (1) pricing is appraisal-driven,
  not provenance-priced; (2) ~13-year average holds mean the sale event almost never arrives;
  (3) there is no owner-maintained, provenance-priced secondary market to reward the records.
- Garage's entire wedge is the sale-time evidence dossier; the asset class has no sale-time
  buyer who pays for owner-authored provenance. `mechanism` 1, `evidence` 0,
  `cost_survival` 0, `implementation_cost` −3 (whole new vertical, permanent tax) ⇒ composite 1.

## Biggest risk
Re-entering a category with a fresh corpse, diluting the vehicle wedge and the adopted
profit-first plan (which lists this in its expansion "Never" row) for zero resale-driven WTP.

## Reconsideration trigger (evidence-dated exclusion, per Sol correction #5)
**Material new evidence of provenance-priced home resale behavior** — i.e., real-world proof
that owner-maintained home records move sale price in a documented secondary market. Absent
that, this ground is dead; do not re-assay.
