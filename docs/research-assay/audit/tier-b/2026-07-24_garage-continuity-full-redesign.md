## Core claim

Adopting the Garage Continuity concept — a four-tab owner-oriented IA
(Today / Ledger / Record / Vehicle), truthful provenance semantics (Record
Stamp origin/evidence/revision, Correction Branch append-only corrections,
Commit-to-Ledger draft ritual for AI/voice/import), a Disclosure-Preview
Dossier as the top monetization surface, an archival "quiet stewardship"
brand system, and contextual (not tab-gated) monetization — improves user
comprehension, trust, and dossier-boundary conversion via making record
origin, evidence, and correction state explicit and owner-controlled.

## Biggest risk

Wholesale adoption conflates two very different bets: a quarter-plus IA+brand
swap on a mature codebase (weak mechanism, unvalidated distinctiveness, real
clearance cost, permanent governance tax) with a small set of high-value,
unbundlable data-semantics and UX pieces that directly serve already-voted
Wave-3 work. Buying the bundle to get the pieces would burn pre-revenue runway
on the unvalidated half while the load-bearing resale-trust premise is still
behind the open kill-test.
