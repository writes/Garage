- Ask-to-Buy/payment-pending resets purchaseState to idle and persists no pending marker, immediately re-enabling Buy. Repeated taps can create duplicate deferred purchases or charges, with no transaction-specific recovery path after approval.
- A successful purchase with no transaction ID performs only one immediate quota refresh. If the grant lands later, no marker, poll, timer, or latch-clearing refresh remains; the UI stays delayed and blocked until the sheet is recreated despite a charge.
- ReceiptCaptureView awaits the uncapped sweepIncomplete loop before creating the controller or resuming markers. A stale true response calls every 5 seconds forever and starves paid-purchase repair and product setup for that sheet.
- At TTL, any successful reconcile returning transactionState=unknown is treated as authoritative absence and deletes the only transaction key. Since unknown is also nonterminal, a slow ledger can be orphaned and Buy re-enabled without definitive not-found proof.
