### Blocking findings

- Identity is not serialized across purchase: after the last UID check, LiveReceiptCreditsStoreClient awaits another product fetch, then calls StoreKit. An account switch can bind/grant to another RevenueCat user while the marker is saved under the pre-check UID.
- A successful RevenueCat purchase with no transaction ID performs one quota refresh but stores no marker. If the grant is delayed or refresh fails, dismissal/relaunch loses all repair and suppression state and can re-offer the consumable to an already charged user.
- Ask-to-Buy/SCA pending is set back to idle with no durable pending record, immediately re-enabling Buy while approval is outstanding. A repeat can create another pending or charged consumable; keep pending state and reconcile before re-offering.
- Every non-cancel/pending RevenueCat error becomes storeError then idle. Errors after StoreKit interaction can be indeterminate; without transaction/status reconciliation, the client treats 'unknown whether charged' as safe to retry and can double-charge.
- Offer suppression is asynchronous: offerContext never checks queued markers, and product availability becomes true before resumeOutstandingMarkers finishes. During a slow or failed repair, a quota denial can expose Buy despite an unresolved paid transaction.

### Advisory findings

- With creditsDeficit > 0, the CTA still promises 'Add 10 receipt saves' although some or all 10 are withheld; a deficit of 10+ can charge for zero usable saves. Make the exact net outcome explicit to satisfy [Apple 2.3.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata).
- All UIDs and transaction repair keys share one unencrypted UserDefaults JSON blob; any decode failure silently resets the entire queue. One corrupt/older value can erase every account's paid-purchase recovery state. Use resilient per-record storage and surface failures.
- sweepIncomplete retries forever at the 5-second ceiling. A stale server flag keeps the sheet task and Functions traffic alive indefinitely; add a bounded elapsed-time/circuit-breaker path that preserves paid balances and reports degraded state.
- No executed build/test evidence is supplied. The named Firebase UID-change test does not switch identity during the awaited purchase; it changes UID before a second call, leaving the money-path race untested. Pending, ambiguous-error, and persistence recovery are also unproved.
- HANDOFF.md is deliberately deferred until after merge, while the committed tri-review report is tied to obsolete SHAs and prior NO-GO findings. Main would lack a HEAD-tied handoff/verdict, contrary to write-last and evidence-provenance doctrine.
