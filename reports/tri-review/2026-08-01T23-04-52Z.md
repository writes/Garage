### Blocking findings

- Ask-to-Buy/SCA `.pending` writes no durable marker and resets the controller to `.idle`. A later approval may charge/grant while Buy is offered again, enabling duplicate consumable purchases without transaction-specific recovery.
- A successful purchase with a nil transaction ID gets one immediate quota refresh and no durable marker. If its grant is delayed or its webhook is lost, reopening forgets suppression and repair state and can offer the customer a second charge.
- The identity check is not a lease: the live client awaits another product fetch and StoreKit after the last check. Firebase or RevenueCat identity can change during that window while the resulting marker remains bound to the pre-check UID.
- At TTL, any successful reconcile returning `unknown` is treated as a definitive miss; the transaction marker is deleted and Buy remains enabled. This discards the repair key and can re-charge a customer whose purchase is merely unobserved.

### Advisory findings

- The `sweepIncomplete` loop has no terminal budget; after backoff it calls every five seconds forever. A stale server flag can drain battery and create unbounded Functions traffic; add a circuit-breaker or bounded foreground policy.
- Grant, refund, delayed, and support UI is nested under `offerContext`, which requires a fetchable product and an enabled purchase flag. During a catalog outage or safety flag-off, paid-purchase status and support guidance disappear.
- No secret is introduced, but Firebase UIDs and transaction IDs live in a silently decoded UserDefaults blob; corruption or schema drift drops every repair marker. Use protected, versioned, fail-closed storage with explicit migration.
- When refund deficit consumes some or all of a pack, the button still promises “Add 10 receipt saves.” Show the exact net usable credits at confirmation so the localized purchase disclosure is not materially misleading.
- Proof and doctrine evidence are stale: no test drives late pending approval, nil-ID relaunch, or identity change inside the awaited store call; HANDOFF names 567a768 while claiming b1d680e's fix, and the committed review targets older SHAs.
