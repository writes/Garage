### Blocking findings

- `ReceiptCreditsPurchaser` rechecks identity, then `LiveReceiptCreditsStoreClient.purchaseCredits()` awaits a second product fetch before StoreKit. Identity can change in that gap, and no lease serializes identity mutation, risking purchase/grant attribution across accounts.
- A successful purchase with a nil transaction ID gets no durable marker. If the immediate quota refresh is early or identity changes, the next sheet can sell again while the first charge/grant is unresolved, and transaction-specific reconciliation is impossible.
- `paymentPending` immediately restores `.idle` and persists nothing. Repeated taps or sheet reopens can create multiple Ask-to-Buy/SCA requests for the consumable; later approvals can produce unintended multiple charges. Pending needs a durable, buy-suppressing state.
- After 72h, a StoreKit-success transaction still reported `unknown` is deleted; only a UID support flag remains and the same UI still shows Buy. Server absence does not prove no charge, so the app can solicit a second purchase while the first is unresolved.
- `ReceiptCaptureView.task` awaits the unbounded `sweepIncomplete` loop before creating the credits controller or resuming markers. A stuck server flag can indefinitely starve repair for already-paid purchases despite the request-rate backoff.

### Advisory findings

- Any non-cancel RevenueCat error maps to `.storeError`, re-enables Buy, and leaves no durable uncertain-purchase record. Errors after StoreKit commitment or receipt handling could therefore permit a second charge.
- All UIDs, transaction IDs, and support flags share one unprotected UserDefaults blob; decode failure silently discards every account's repair queue. This is sensitive purchase metadata and a single-point data-loss path.
- An active unknown marker gets one status plus reconcile on each sheet appearance, then no in-view polling. The UI can remain delayed after the webhook lands, and its Pull to refresh instruction is false because no `.refreshable` action exists.
- The CTA promises Add 10 receipt saves even when `creditsDeficit` consumes some or all of the pack; repeated purchases may yield zero usable saves. Nearby deficit copy reduces but does not remove accuracy and App Store policy risk.
- The manifest has no HANDOFF update or current-head build/test receipt. Added tests and older review reports are not execution proof for `a093fda`, so doctrine compliance and gate evidence remain incomplete.
