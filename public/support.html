Common questions

How do I get my records out?

Open a vehicle, then use Export. You can produce a PDF dossier or a CSV of the full service history. The export is designed to hand to a buyer at resale. Your records are yours — you can take them with you at any time, without a subscription.

What does Pro include?

Pro raises the vehicle limit, unlocks receipt and document attachments, adds AI parsing of service and oil-analysis PDFs, voice quick-add, and accent themes. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. See the Terms of Use for full renewal details.

I paid but Pro is not active

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of purchase and we will sort it out.

How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and its records from our servers. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, so export anything you want to keep first.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Records you add offline are stored on the device and sync when you reconnect. The sync indicator shows what has and has not reached the server yet.
